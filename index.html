Project 2

Project 2 Prototype

The Dining Story is an interactive dining experience that uses Arduino and TouchDesigner to bring food stories to life through projection. Users press a button to choose a dish, and animated visuals spread across the table before coming together on the plate. Each dish has its own visuals and animations that reflect its ingredients and story. This prototype helped me explore how digital storytelling can add another layer to a dining experience.
